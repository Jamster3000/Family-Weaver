# Family Weaver

Family Weaver is a modern, accessible genealogy desktop application that allows you to build and manage your family tree, track ancestors and their relationships, and visualize multi-generational history.

It is intentionally designed from the ground up to be intuitive, distraction-free, and approachable for **seniors, older adults, and non-tech-savvy users**.

### Core Focus
- **Performance** Fast, lightweight and responsive.
- **Simplicity** Clean navigation with zero unnecessary UI clutter.
- **Accessibility** Fully accessible to users with disabilities.

---

## Project Motivation & Inspiration

Family Weaver was born out of frustration with existing genealogy tools used by myself and my mum. Most mainstream websites and desktop software in this space are 10 5o 15 years old, some in which feel clunky and present significant pain points for everyday users:

- **Paywalls & Subscriptions:** Core tools are frequently locked behind expensive recurring fees.
- **Privacy Concern:** Your family history is intimate data-many online platforms act as data brokers (data sold, used for improving systems, etc.) behind a fancy interface
- **Overcomplicated Interfaces:** Confusing navigation and steep learning curves make mapping a single family tree feel overwhelming.
- **Poor Accessibility:** Essential features are often illegible or difficult to use for seniors and users with visual or physical impairments.
- **Data Lock-in:** Limited or poor export and printing capabilities make it hard to truly own your family tree.

Family Weaver exists to solve these problems by providing a practical, modern, privacy-focused alternative with features people actually need all entirely free.

---

## Key Features

### Visual Family Tree
- **Interactive Canvas:** Effortlessly navigate and pan through your multi-generational tree with an entirely custom designed tree rendering.
- **Family Trees:** Create and manage multiple family trees, for different families or surnames.

### Detailed Person Profiles
* **Rich Biographical Data:** Record names, vital dates (birth, marriage, death), and personal notes.

### Privacy & Local
**Complete Data Ownership:** All data stays locally on your device - no accounts, no cloud tracking, absolutely no hidden fees.
**Offline Access:** Family Weaver software requires no internet at all.

### Designed for Senior Accessibility
- **Legible & Scaled UI:** High-contrast, colour blind modes, clear typography, make UI larger, and much more.
